Questions

Before you press enter

Do I need to own a mechanical keyboard?

No. Plenty of members join before their first build. Switch tier is built for exactly that, and the beginner build night walks you through choosing your first board.

When do drops ship?

Drops ship on the 1st of each month to Keycap and Custom members. Join before the 28th to get that month's drop.

Can I pause or cancel?

Anytime, from your account page. You can pause for up to three months and keep your member number.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, worldwide. Shipping is included on drops. Group-buy shipping depends on the vendor.

Can I change tiers later?

Yes. Upgrades apply straight away, and downgrades take effect at your next billing date.
